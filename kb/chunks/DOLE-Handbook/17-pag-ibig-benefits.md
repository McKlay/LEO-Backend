# Pag-IBIG Benefits: Housing, Savings, and Loan Programs

This section covers the Home Development Mutual Fund (Pag-IBIG Fund) programs and benefits, including savings facilities, short-term loans, housing financing, and membership contribution schedules.

---

## 19. PAG-IBIG BENEFITS (RA 9679)

### A. Home Development Mutual Fund

The **Home Development Mutual Fund (HDMF)**, also known as **Pag-IBIG** (*Pagtutulungan sa Kinabukasan: Ikaw, Bangko, Industriya at Goberyno* - Partnership for the Future: You, Bank, Industry and Government) Fund, is a **Government-Owned and Controlled Corporation (GOCC)** that provides the Filipino workers:

- Access to a **secured savings**, AND
- **Affordable home financing** to improve the quality of their lives

### B. Coverage

**Pag-IBIG membership is mandatory** for:

1. **All employees covered by the Social Security System (SSS)** and the **Government Service Insurance System (GSIS)**;

2. **Uniformed members** of:
   - Armed Forces of the Philippines (AFP)
   - Bureau of Fire Protection (BFP)
   - Bureau of Jail Management and Penology (BJMP)
   - Philippine National Police (PNP)

3. **Filipinos employed by foreign-based employers**

### C. Programs and Benefits

The Fund offers its members the following programs and benefits:

#### 1. Provident Savings

##### a. Pag-IBIG Regular Savings

The **Pag-IBIG Regular Savings**, or the **monthly mandatory membership savings**, is a savings facility that gives members **high annual dividend earnings**.

**How Much to Contribute:**

- **Members who are formally employed:**  
  Contribute a minimum of **₱100** from their monthly income

- **Employer's Counterpart:**  
  Employers also match their contributions with a share of **₱100**

- **Self-employed members:**  
  Can contribute for as low as **₱100 per month**

**When to Withdraw:**

Members can withdraw their Pag-IBIG Savings and its dividend earnings upon:
- **Retirement**, OR
- **After 20 years**, OR
- **Equivalent to 240 monthly contributions**

##### b. Modified Pag-IBIG 2 (MP2) Savings

A **special voluntary savings facility** that is open to all active Pag-IBIG Members who wish to **save more and earn higher dividends**, in addition to their Pag-IBIG Regular Savings.

**Key Features:**

- **Easy and flexible** savings program
- **5-year maturity period**
- Members can start saving for as low as **₱500 per remittance**, making it more accessible
- The **dividends earned in the MP2 Savings are tax-free**
- Dividends can be withdrawn **annually** or **after the 5-year maturity period**

#### 2. Short-Term Loans

##### a. Pag-IBIG Multi-Purpose Loan

An **affordable and easily accessible cash loan** program offered by Pag-IBIG Fund.

**Loan Amount:**
- Qualified members can borrow up to **80% of their total Pag-IBIG Regular Savings**, which includes:
  - Their monthly contributions
  - Employer's contributions
  - Accumulated dividends earned

**Use of Loan Proceeds:**
- Pay for tuition fees
- Medical expenses
- Minor home improvements
- Travel
- Capital for a small business

**Repayment Terms:**
- Payable in **24 or 36 monthly installments**
- Low interest rate of **10.5% per year**

##### b. Pag-IBIG Calamity Loan

The **Pag-IBIG Calamity Loan** aims to provide immediate financial assistance to Pag-IBIG members who have been affected by calamities and are residing in **areas declared under a State of Calamity**.

**Loan Amount:**
- Eligible members may borrow up to **80% of their total Pag-IBIG Savings**, which consist of:
  - Their monthly contributions
  - The counterpart employer's contributions
  - Accumulated dividends earned

**Terms:**
- Offered at a rate of **5.95% per annum** (the lowest rate in the market)
- Payable over a period of **up to three years**
- With a **grace period of three months**

#### 3. Home Financing

##### a. Retail Housing Loans

###### i. Pag-IBIG Housing Loan

Allows Pag-IBIG members to borrow:
- **Up to ₱6 million** at low interest rates
- With borrower-friendly terms
- Maximum repayment period of **up to 30 years**

###### ii. Pag-IBIG Affordable Housing Loan for Minimum-Wage Earners

Designed for **minimum-wage and low-income members** who:
- Earn **up to ₱15,000 a month** within the National Capital Region (NCR), AND
- Earn **up to ₱12,000 per month** outside the NCR

**Special Features:**

Under the loan program, Pag-IBIG Fund offers:
- A **subsidized rate of 3% per annum** for home loans up to **₱580,000** in socialized subdivision projects
- **Special low rates** for home loans of up to **₱750,000** for socialized condominium projects

###### iii. Pag-IBIG Home Equity Appreciation Loan (HEAL)

A **low-interest loan** available to all members with updated payments for their Pag-IBIG Housing Loan.

**Purpose:**
- Members can borrow an amount based on the **net value of their mortgaged property** with Pag-IBIG Fund to help them fund their life goals:
  - Renovate their homes
  - Buy a car
  - Purchase furniture and appliances

###### iv. Pag-IBIG Home Saver Programs

Provides **options to Pag-IBIG Housing Loan borrowers** who have missed payments to:
- Update their accounts, AND
- Save their homes from foreclosure

##### b. Institutional Housing Financing

###### i. Direct Developmental Loan (DDL)

Provides **loan financing for property/real-estate developers/proponents** to develop:
- Residential subdivisions or medium-rise residential buildings, AND
- Housing units eligible for mortgage financing under Pag-IBIG Housing Loan

###### ii. Direct Developmental Loan Program for the Pambansang Pabahay Para sa Pilipino Housing (4PH) Program [DDLP-4PH]

**Encourages public-private partnership** by providing access to affordable financing mechanism for:
- Property developers
- Local Government Units (LGUs)
- Contractors
- Joint venture entities

**For the development of:**
- Residential subdivision/condominium housing projects, AND
- Construction of housing units

**Aims to:**
- Support the national government and the **Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development (DHSUD)**
- Reduce the **6 million housing backlog** of the country

###### iii. Rental Housing Construction Loan (RHCL)

A **financing facility** which may be used to fund:
- The **site development** and **construction** of residential units meant for rental housing
- Which may include:
  - Staff houses
  - Dormitories
  - Other similar dwellings

**Open to:**
- Employers, whether **private** or from the **government sector**

#### 4. Other Programs and Benefits

##### a. Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card Plus

The **Pag-IBIG Loyalty Card Plus** is a **discounts and rewards card** that allows members to enjoy exclusive offers from **more than 380 Loyalty Card partner-establishments nationwide**.

**Benefits:**
- Get **discounts and reward points** on:
  - Grocery purchases
  - Furniture upgrades
  - Tuition fees
  - Hospital bills
  - Fuel expenses
  - And many more

**Additional Features:**
- Can be used as a **Savings Account**
- A means for members to receive their **savings claims and loan proceeds** more conveniently

##### b. Virtual Pag-IBIG for Members

An **online platform** that allows members to access Pag-IBIG services **24/7, anytime, anywhere**.

**Includes:**
- A **24/7 chat service** where members can go to for their inquiries and concerns

##### c. Virtual Pag-IBIG for Developers

An **online facility** that aids partner-developers in **evaluating borrowers' eligibility**.

**Benefits:**
- Through the Virtual Pag-IBIG for Developers, Pag-IBIG Fund is able to **digitize and expedite housing loan applications**

##### d. Virtual Pag-IBIG for Employers

The **Virtual Pag-IBIG for Employers** is an online facility that allows employers to enjoy Pag-IBIG Fund services for their businesses and employees **safely and conveniently**.

##### e. Virtual Pag-IBIG Mobile App

The **official mobile application** of Pag-IBIG Fund that aims to bring its services closer to the members.

**With just a few taps on their smartphone and with the internet, members can conveniently:**
- Access Pag-IBIG Fund's services
- Keep track of their **savings and loan records**
- Make **online payments for their dues**

### D. Membership Contribution

**Contribution Rate Schedule:**

| Monthly Compensation | Contribution Rate |  |
|---------------------|------------------|------------------|
|  | **Employee** | **Employer (if any)** |
| **₱1,500 and below** | 1.00% | 2.00% |
| **Over ₱1,500** | 2.00% | 2.00% |

**What is "Monthly Compensation"?**

"Monthly Compensation" shall refer to the **basic salary and other allowances**, where basic salary includes, but is not limited to:
- Fees
- Salaries
- Wages
- Similar items received in a month

It shall mean the **remuneration or earnings**, however designated, capable of being expressed in terms of money, whether:
- Fixed or ascertained on a time, task, or piece or commission basis, or other method of calculating the same

Which is payable by an employer to an employee or by one person to another under a written or unwritten contract of employment for work done or to be done, or for services rendered or to be rendered.

**Maximum Monthly Compensation:**

The **maximum monthly compensation** to be used in computing the employee and employer contributions shall **not be more than ₱5,000**.

**Employer's Obligations:**

- Employers shall remit **two percent (2%)** of the monthly compensation of the contributing member as **counterpart contribution**

- The employer is **not entitled to deduct** from the wages or remuneration of or, otherwise, to recover from the employee the **employer's contribution**

**Special Provision for Full-Time Spouses:**

Full-time spouses who volunteer to be covered by Pag-IBIG Fund membership shall adopt as a basis of monthly contributions **one-half (½) of the monthly compensation income of the employed spouse**.

The full-time spouse shall **not be required to pay the employer's contribution**.

**Members with Multiple Employers:**

In cases where a member has **two or more employers**, such member shall contribute monthly to the Fund a percentage of his or her monthly compensation **per employer**, which shall be **matched by the latter**.
