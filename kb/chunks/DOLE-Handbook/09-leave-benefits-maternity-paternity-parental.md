# Leave Benefits: Maternity, Paternity, and Parental Leave for Solo Parents

This section covers three major leave benefits that support employees during childbirth and child-rearing: maternity leave for female workers, paternity leave for married male employees, and parental leave for solo parents.

---

## 8. MATERNITY LEAVE (RA 11210)

### A. Definition

Maternity leave refers to the paid leave granted to a qualified female worker in connection with childbirth, miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy to enable her to give her full attention to her special maternal functions during this critical stage.

### B. Coverage

This benefit applies to all female workers in the private sector, irrespective of their civil status and regardless of whether or not they are married, who give birth or experience miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy.

### C. Duration

Under Republic Act No. 11210 (Expanded Maternity Leave Law), female workers are entitled to:

- **105 days of paid maternity leave** (base entitlement)
- **Additional 15 days for solo mothers** (total 120 days)
- **Optional 30 days extension without pay** (bringing potential total to 135/150 days)

**RA 11210 Background:**
- Enacted: February 20, 2019
- Published: Manila Bulletin, February 23, 2019
- Effective: March 11, 2019
- Implementing Rules and Regulations: May 1, 2019

**Allocation of the 105-day Paid Leave:**
The 105-day maternity leave period may be extended according to the female worker's preference, before or after childbirth or miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy. However, at least fifteen (15) calendar days of the one hundred five (105)-day paid maternity leave period must be taken after childbirth or pregnancy termination.

**Additional 30-Day Optional Extension (Unpaid):**
After the initial 105-day period (or 120 days for solo mothers), the female worker may opt to extend for an additional thirty (30) days without pay. However, she is not precluded from returning to work prior to the expiration of the said optional extension.

The thirty (30) days extension may be availed of in case the female worker has completed the full paid maternity leave of one hundred five (105) days or one hundred twenty (120) days for solo mothers.

The female worker may avail of any working arrangement agreed upon with her employer during the optional extension, provided it is consented to in writing by the female worker and primarily upholds her maternal functions and the requirements of postnatal care.

### D. Conditions for Entitlement

To qualify for the maternity leave, the female employee must:

1. Have at least **three (3) monthly contributions to the SSS** in the twelve-month period immediately preceding the semester of childbirth, miscarriage, or emergency termination of pregnancy; and
2. Have **notified her employer** of her pregnancy and the probable date of her childbirth, which notice shall be transmitted to SSS in accordance with its rules and regulations.

> **Note:** For SSS procedures, view the step-by-step video illustration at: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhwsbRR6OXc

### E. Frequency of Availment

Maternity leave shall be granted to a qualified female worker in **every instance** of pregnancy, miscarriage or emergency termination of pregnancy **regardless of frequency**.

**Salary Differential During Maternity Leave:**
- See DOLE Department Advisory No. 1, Series of 2019: Guidelines on the Computation of Salary Differential of Female Workers during her Maternity Leave and its Criteria for Exemption Pursuant to RA 11210 and its IRR (issued July 9, 2019)

## 9. PATERNITY LEAVE (RA 8187)

### A. Definition

Paternity leave refers to the paid leave granted to a married male employee allowing him to lend support to his lawful wife during her period of recovery and/or in nursing of their newborn child, including miscarriage.

### B. Coverage

This benefit applies to **all married male employees**, regardless of their employment status (e.g., probationary, regular, contractual, project basis).

### C. Duration

The paternity leave grants male employees **seven (7) days** of full pay during which he is allowed not to report for work.

**Timing:**
- Granted after the delivery by his wife
- Employer may allow availment before or during delivery
- **Total shall not exceed 7 days for each delivery**

**Non-Convertibility:**
If the paternity leave is not availed of, said leave shall **not be convertible to cash**.

**Coordination with Existing Benefits:**
Where a male employee is already enjoying paternity leave benefits by reason of contract, company policy or collective bargaining agreement:

a. If the existing paternity leave benefit is **greater** than the benefit provided by RA 8187, the **greater benefit shall prevail**; and
b. If the existing paternity leave is **less** than 7 days, such existing benefit shall be **adjusted to the extent of the difference**.

**Emergency Leave Distinction:**
Where a contract, company policy or CBA provides for an emergency or contingency leave **without specific provisions on paternity leave**, the paternity leave as provided in RA 8187 shall **apply in full**.

### D. Conditions for Entitlement

A married male employee shall be entitled to paternity leave benefit provided that he has met the following conditions:

1. He is an **employee at the time of delivery** of his child;
2. He is **cohabiting with his spouse** at the time that she gives birth or suffers a miscarriage;
3. He has **applied for paternity leave** with his employer within a reasonable period of time from the expected date of delivery by his pregnant spouse, or within such period as may be provided by company rules and regulations or by collective bargaining agreement, provided that **prior application for leave shall not be required in case of miscarriage**; and
4. His wife has **given birth or suffered a miscarriage**.

**Definition of "Cohabiting":**
"Cohabiting" means the obligation of the husband and wife to live together. **If the spouses are not physically living together because of their workstation or occupation, the male employee is still entitled to the paternity leave benefit.**

### E. Frequency of Availment

Paternity leave benefit shall apply to the **first four (4) deliveries** of the employee's lawful wife with whom he is cohabiting.

## 10. PARENTAL LEAVE FOR SOLO PARENTS (RA 8972, as amended by RA 11861)

### A. Definition

Parental leave refers to the paid leave granted to a qualified solo parent to enable the performance of parental duties and responsibilities where physical presence is required or beneficial to the child.

**Legislative Background:**
- Original Law: RA 8972
- Amendment: **RA 11861 ("Expanded Solo Parents Welfare Act")**
  - Enacted: June 4, 2022
  - Published: Daily Tribune, June 30, 2022
  - Effective: July 15, 2022

### B. Coverage

This benefit applies to all employees who belong to the following categories of solo parents:

**1. Parent providing sole parental care and support due to:**
   - a. **Birth as a consequence of rape** (even without final conviction); mother has sole parental care
   - b. **Death of the spouse**
   - c. **Detention of the spouse** for at least 3 months or service of sentence
   - d. **Physical or mental incapacity of the spouse** (certified by medical practitioner)
   - e. **Legal separation or de facto separation** for at least 6 months; solo parent has sole care
   - f. **Declaration of nullity or annulment of marriage**, or divorce; solo parent has sole care
   - g. **Abandonment by the spouse** for at least 6 months

**2. OFW Family Members:**
   - Spouse or family member of an **Overseas Filipino Worker (OFW)**, or guardian of OFW's children
   - **Requirements:**
     - OFW belongs to low/semi-skilled worker category
     - Away from Philippines for uninterrupted period of **12 months**
     - Falls under requirements of this section

**3. Unmarried Parent:**
   - Unmarried mother or father who keeps and rears the child or children

**4. Legal Guardian:**
   - Any legal guardian, adoptive or foster parent who solely provides parental care and support

**5. Relative Caregiver:**
   - Any relative within the **4th civil degree of consanguinity or affinity**
   - Assumes care due to death, abandonment, disappearance or absence of parents/solo parent for at least 6 months
   - **Special provision for solo grandparents who are senior citizens:** entitled to RA 8972 benefits in addition to RA 9257 (Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2003) benefits

**6. Pregnant Woman:**
   - A pregnant woman who provides sole parental care and support to the unborn child or children

**Key Definitions:**

- **"Child" or "dependents":** Those living with and dependent upon the solo parent for support who are:
  - Unmarried, unemployed and 22 years old or below, OR
  - Over 22 years old but unable to fully take care or protect themselves from abuse, neglect, cruelty, exploitation, or discrimination because of a physical or mental disability or condition

- **"Parental care and support":** Acts of providing for the basic needs, health care, mental and physical safety, emotional support and formation of the personality of the child

### C. Duration

In addition to leave privileges under existing laws, a **forfeitable and non-cumulative parental leave of not more than seven (7) working days with pay every year** shall be granted to any solo parent employee, regardless of employment status, who has rendered service of at least six (6) months.

**Important Clarifications:**

- **Shared parental support disqualifies:** A solo parent shall not lose his or her status as solo parent if the other parent provides occasional assistance and/or seasonal gifts that do not meet the legal requirement of support under The Family Code of the Philippines. However, the absence of a valid and legal marriage between the mother and father of a child does not automatically entitle either individual to the benefits if the factual circumstances demonstrate that parental care and support are **shared**.

- **Non-Convertibility:** In the event that the parental leave is not availed of, said leave shall **not be convertible to cash** unless specifically agreed upon previously by the employer and the employee.

- **Coordination with Existing Benefits:**
  - If there is an existing or similar benefit under a company policy or a CBA, the same shall be credited as such
  - If the same is greater than 7 days, the **greater benefit shall prevail**
  - **Emergency or contingency leave** provided under a company policy or CBA shall **not be credited** as compliance with the parental leave

### D. Conditions for Entitlement

A solo parent shall be entitled to the parental leave provided that:

1. They have rendered at least **six (6) months of service**, whether continuous or broken, at the time of the effectivity of RA 11861 (July 15, 2022);
2. They have **notified their employer** of the availment thereof within a reasonable time; and
3. They have presented a **valid Solo Parent Identification Card (SPIC)**.

**How to Obtain SPIC:**
Qualified solo parents may apply for their SPIC at the **Social Welfare and Development Officer** of the city or municipality of their residence.

### E. Frequency of Availment

The parental leave of **seven (7) working days with pay shall be granted every year**.

**Cessation of Solo Parent Status:**
When a solo parent ceases to be such by reason of change of status and circumstances, the said solo parent shall be **ineligible to avail of the solo parent benefits**.
