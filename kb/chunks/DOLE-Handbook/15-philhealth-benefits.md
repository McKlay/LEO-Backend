# PhilHealth Benefits: Universal Health Care Coverage and Contributions

This section covers the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) benefits under the National Health Insurance Program, including universal health care coverage, benefit packages, and premium contribution schedules.

---

## 17. PHILHEALTH BENEFITS (RA 7875, as amended by RA 9241 and RA 10606)

### A. National Health Insurance Program

The **National Health Insurance Program (NHIP)** is a **compulsory health insurance program** of the government established to provide:
- **Universal health insurance coverage**, AND
- Ensure **affordable, acceptable, available and accessible health care services** for all citizens of the Philippines

### B. Coverage

The NHIP covers **all Filipinos** pursuant to **Republic Act No. 11223** or the **Universal Health Care (UHC) Act**.

### C. Benefits

For detailed information on PhilHealth benefits, refer to the official PhilHealth website:

🔗 **https://www.philhealth.gov.ph/benefits/**

**Benefit Categories:**

1. **Inpatient Benefits**
2. **Outpatient Benefits**
3. **Z Benefits**
4. **SDG Related Benefits**

**Important Note on 2023 Premium Rate:**

For **2023**, premium rate **remains at 4%**. In a Statement released on **January 4, 2023**, the Board of Directors of the PhilHealth affirmed its compliance with the **directive from the President of the Philippines** to:
- **Suspend** the premium rate increase from 4.0% to 4.5%, AND
- **Suspend** the increase of income ceiling from ₱80,000.00 to ₱90,000.00 in CY 2023

**Who are Direct Contributors?**

Direct Contributors are the following:

1. **Employees with formal employment**;
2. **Kasambahays** (domestic workers);
3. **Self-earning individuals and professional practitioners**;
4. **Overseas Filipino Workers (OFWs)**;
5. **Filipinos living abroad and those with dual citizenship**;
6. **Lifetime members**; and
7. **All Filipinos aged 21 years and above with capacity to pay**.

**How Premium is Computed:**

The premium of employed members in the government and private sector, including household help and sea-based OFWs, shall be computed **straight based on the monthly basic salary** of the employee to be **equally shared between the employee and employer**.

**Income Floor and Ceiling Rules:**

- **For those whose basic monthly salary is below the income floor:**  
  The premium contribution shall be computed **based on the income floor**

- **For those whose basic monthly income is above the income ceiling:**  
  The premium contribution shall be computed **based on the income ceiling**

This is in accordance with the **DBM Circular Letter No. 2019-8**.

**Employer Responsibility During Maternity Leave:**

Employers shall ensure that **premium contribution of women who are on maternity leave shall be regularly remitted to PhilHealth** pursuant to **DOLE Department Advisory No. 1, Series of 2019**.
