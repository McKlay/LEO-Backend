# Thirteenth-Month Pay: Computation, Coverage, and Tax Treatment

This section covers the mandatory thirteenth-month pay benefit for rank-and-file employees, including computation methods, coverage rules, special cases, and tax treatment under the TRAIN law.

---

## 13. THIRTEENTH-MONTH PAY (PD 851)

### A. Definition

**Thirteenth-month pay** refers to **one-twelfth (1/12) of the total basic salary** earned by an employee within a calendar year.

### B. Coverage

**General Rule:**
All employers are required to pay their **rank-and-file employees** thirteenth-month pay, regardless of:
- The nature of their employment, and
- The methods by which their wages are paid

**Minimum Service Requirement:**
Employees must have worked for **at least one (1) month during a calendar year**.

**Who are Rank-and-File Employees?**

The Labor Code distinguishes between:

- **Managerial employee:** One who is vested with powers or prerogatives to:
  - Lay down and execute management policies, and/or
  - Hire, transfer, suspend, layoff, recall, discharge, assign, or discipline employees, or
  - Effectively recommend such managerial actions

- **Rank-and-file employees:** All employees **not falling within the managerial employee definition**

> **Note:** The above distinction shall be used as guide for the purpose of determining who are rank-and-file employees entitled to the thirteenth-month pay.

**Employers NOT Covered by PD 851:**

The following employers are **not required** to pay thirteenth-month pay:

1. **Government and political subdivisions**, including government-owned and controlled corporations, **except** those corporations operating essentially as private subsidiaries of the government;

2. **Employers already paying** their employees a thirteenth-month pay or more in a calendar year or its equivalent at the time of the issuance of PD 851;

   > **"Its equivalent"** shall include:
   > - Christmas bonus
   > - Mid-year bonus
   > - Cash bonuses
   > - Other payments amounting to not less than 1/12 of the basic salary
   > 
   > **"Its equivalent" does NOT include:**
   > - Cash and stock dividends
   > - Cost of living allowance (COLA)
   > - All other allowances regularly enjoyed by the employee
   > - Non-monetary benefits

3. **Employers of household helpers** and persons in the personal service of another in relation to such workers; and

4. **Employers of workers paid on:**
   - Purely commission basis
   - Boundary basis
   - Task basis
   - Fixed amount for performing specific work, irrespective of time consumed

   **EXCEPTION:** Employers shall grant thirteenth-month pay to **workers paid on piece-rate basis**.

   > **"Workers paid on piece-rate basis"** refer to those who are paid a standard amount for every piece or unit of work produced that is more or less regularly replicated, **without regard to the time spent** in producing the same.

### C. Amount

The thirteenth-month pay shall **not be less than one-twelfth (1/12) of the total basic salary** earned by an employee in a calendar year.

**What is "Basic Salary"?**

The "basic salary" of an employee for the purpose of computing the thirteenth-month pay shall include:
- ✅ **All remunerations or earnings paid** by his or her employer for services rendered

The basic salary **does NOT include** allowances and monetary benefits which are not considered or integrated as part of the regular or basic salary, such as:
- ❌ Cash equivalent of unused vacation and sick leave credits
- ❌ Overtime pay
- ❌ Premium pay
- ❌ Night shift differential
- ❌ Holiday pay
- ❌ Cost of living allowance (COLA)

**IMPORTANT EXCEPTION:**
These salary-related benefits **should be included as part of the basic salary** in the computation of the thirteenth-month pay **if these are treated as part of the basic salary** of the employees, through:
- Individual or collective agreement
- Company practice or policy

### D. Computation

**Formula:**

```
Total basic salary earned during the year
─────────────────────────────────────── = Proportionate 13th-month pay
              12 months
```

**Illustration:**

Using the daily minimum wage in the National Capital Region:
- **₱570.00** from January 1 to July 15, 2023
- **₱610.00** from July 16 to December 31, 2023

For a **six-day workweek**:

**Monthly Basic Salary Computation:**

From Jan 1 - July 15:
```
₱570 × 313 days
───────────── = ₱14,867.50
  12 months
```

From July 16 - Dec 31:
```
₱610 × 313 days
───────────── = ₱15,910.83
  12 months
```

**Monthly Breakdown with Absences:**

| Month      | Status                  | Monthly Salary  |
|------------|-------------------------|-----------------|
| January    | No absence              | ₱ 14,867.50     |
| February   | No absence              | 14,867.50       |
| March      | No absence              | 14,867.50       |
| April      | No absence              | 14,867.50       |
| May        | No absence              | 14,867.50       |
| June       | 5 days leave with pay   | 14,867.50       |
| July       | No absence              | 15,389.17       |
| August     | No absence              | 15,910.83       |
| September  | 10 days leave w/o pay   | 9,810.83        |
| October    | No absence              | 15,910.83       |
| November   | 1 day leave w/o pay     | 15,300.83       |
| December   | No absence              | 15,910.83       |
| **TOTAL**  |                         | **₱177,438.32** |

**13th-Month Pay Computation:**

```
₱177,438.32
─────────── = ₱14,786.53 (proportionate 13th-month pay)
  12 months
```

### E. Time of Payment

The thirteenth-month pay shall be paid **not later than December 24** of every year.

**Split Payment Option:**
An employer may give to his or her employees:
- **One-half (1/2)** of the thirteenth-month pay **before the opening of the regular school year**, and
- **Remaining half** on or **before December 24** of every year

**Negotiated Payment Schedule:**
The frequency of payment of this monetary benefit may be the **subject of an agreement** between the employer and the recognized/collective bargaining agent of the employees.

### F. Thirteenth-Month Pay for Certain Types of Employees

#### 1. Employees Paid on Piecework Basis

Employees who are paid on piecework basis **are entitled** to the thirteenth-month pay.

#### 2. Employees Paid Fixed/Guaranteed Wage Plus Commission

Employees who are paid a fixed or guaranteed wage plus commission **are also entitled** to the thirteenth-month pay, based on their earnings during the calendar year (i.e., on **both** their fixed or guaranteed wage **and** commission).

**HOWEVER**, the Supreme Court in *Boie-Takeda Chemicals, Inc. vs. De la Serna* (G.R. No. 92174 & G.R. No. 102552, December 10, 1993) ruled that:

> **Commissions, while included in the generic term wage, are not part of "basic salary/wage"** and therefore should not be included in computing the thirteenth-month pay.

The Court stated:

> *"In remunerative schemes consisting of a fixed or guaranteed wage plus commission, the fixed or guaranteed wage is patently the "basic salary" for this is what the employee receives for a standard work period. Commissions are given for extra efforts exerted in consummating sales or other related transactions. They are, as such, additional pay, which this Court has made clear do not form part of the "basic salary"."*

#### 3. Employees with Multiple Employers

**Government employees working part-time in a private enterprise**, including private educational institutions, as well as **employees working in two or more private firms**, whether on full-time or part-time basis, are entitled to the thirteenth-month pay from **all their private employers**, regardless of their total earnings from each of their employers.

### G. Thirteenth-Month Pay of Resigned or Separated Employee

An employee who has **resigned** or whose services are **terminated** at any time before the time of payment of the thirteenth-month pay is entitled to this monetary benefit in **proportion to the length of time** he or she has worked during the year, reckoned from the time he or she has started working during the calendar year up to the time of his or her resignation or termination from the service.

**Example:**
If an employee worked only from **January to September**, his or her proportionate thirteenth-month pay should be equal to **one-twelfth (1/12) of his or her total basic salary earned during that period**.

### H. Thirteenth-Month Pay of Female Employee who is on Maternity Leave

The **salary differential** shall be **included as part of the basic salary** for purposes of computation of the 13th-month pay of the rank-and-file employee.

> **Reference:** DOLE Department Advisory No. 01, Series of 2019 - Guidelines on the Computation of Salary Differential of Female Workers during her Maternity Leave and its Criteria for Exemption Pursuant to RA 11210 and its IRR (issued July 9, 2019)

### I. Non-inclusion in Regular Wage

The thirteenth-month pay is **not part of the regular wage** of employees for purposes of determining:
- Overtime and premium payments
- Fringe benefits
- Contributions to the State Insurance Fund
- Social Security System contributions
- National Health Insurance Program contributions
- Private retirement plans

### J. Coverage from Income Tax of Thirteenth-Month Pay and Other Benefits

**Legal Basis:**
- BIR Revenue Regulation No. 2-98, as amended by RR No. 11-2018
- Section 2.78.1(B)(11)
- Implements TRAIN (Tax Reform for Acceleration and Inclusion) Law
- Issued: January 31, 2018

**Tax-Exempt Benefits:**

The following are **exempted from the requirement of withholding tax on compensation** but may be subject to income tax depending on the nature/sources of income earned by the individual recipient:

**(a) Thirteenth-month pay** equivalent to the mandatory one (1) month basic salary of officials and employees of:
- Government (national or local), including GOCCs, and/or
- Private offices

Received **after the twelfth month pay**.

**(b) Other benefits** such as:
- Christmas bonus
- Productivity incentives
- Loyalty award
- Gift in cash or in kind
- Other benefits of similar nature

Actually received by officials and employees of both government and private offices, including:
- **Additional Compensation Allowance (ACA)** granted and paid to all officials and employees of:
  - National Government Agencies (NGAs)
  - State Universities and Colleges (SUCs)
  - Government-Owned and/or Controlled Corporations (GOCCs)
  - Government Financial Institutions (GFIs)
  - Local Government Units (LGUs)

**Tax Exemption Limit:**

The above stated exclusions under (a) and (b) shall cover benefits paid or accrued during the year, provided that the **total amount shall not exceed ninety thousand pesos (₱90,000.00)**.

> This amount may be increased through rules and regulations issued by the Secretary of Finance, upon recommendation of the Commissioner, after considering among others, the effect on the same of the inflation rate at the end of the taxable year.

### K. No Exemption or Deferment

**No request or application for exemption** from payment of 13th-month pay, or for **deferment of the payment** thereof shall be accepted and allowed.

### L. Report of Compliance

Employers shall make a **report of their compliance** via **online** through the **DOLE Establishment Report System** at:

🔗 **https://reports.dole.gov.ph/**

**Deadline:** Not later than **January 15 of the following year**

**Required Information:**

1. Name of establishment
2. Address
3. Principal product of business
4. Total employment
5. Total number of workers benefitted
6. Amount granted per employee
7. Total amount of benefits granted
8. Name, position and telephone number of person giving information
