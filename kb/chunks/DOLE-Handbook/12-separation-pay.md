# Separation Pay: Coverage, Computation, and Authorized Causes

This section covers separation pay, a compensation given to employees who are terminated from employment due to authorized causes under Articles 298 and 299 of the Labor Code.

---

## 14. SEPARATION PAY (Articles 298-299)

### A. Definition

**Separation pay** is a compensation given to an employee who is terminated from employment due to **authorized causes** provided in Articles 298 and 299 of the Labor Code of the Philippines, as renumbered.

> **Note:** Articles 298-299 were formerly Articles 283-284 prior to renumbering pursuant to DOLE Department Advisory No. 1, Series of 2015 (issued July 21, 2015).

### B. Coverage

This benefit applies to an employee in instances of **authorized termination of employment** covered by Articles 298 and 299 of the Labor Code of the Philippines.

**Important Distinction:**
An employee's entitlement to separation pay **depends on the reason or ground** for the termination of his or her services.

- **Authorized causes** → Separation pay is DUE
- **Just causes** (employee fault) → Separation pay is generally NOT due

### C. Amount

The amount of separation pay varies depending on the specific authorized cause for termination:

#### 1. One-Half (1/2) Month Pay per Year of Service

An employee is entitled to receive separation pay equivalent to **one-half (1/2) month pay for every year of service** (a fraction of at least six (6) months being considered as one (1) whole year), if his/her separation from the service is due to any of the following authorized causes:

**a. Retrenchment to prevent losses**
   - Reduction of personnel effected by management to prevent losses

**b. Closure or cessation of operation**
   - Not due to serious losses or financial reverses

**c. Disease**
   - When the employee is suffering from a disease not curable within a period of six (6) months
   - Continued employment is prejudicial to:
     - His/her health, OR
     - The health of his/her co-employees

**d. Lack of service assignment of security guard**
   - For a continuous period of **six (6) months**
   - See DOLE Department Order No. 150, Series of 2016: Revised Guidelines Governing the Employment and Working Conditions of Security Guards and Other Private Security Personnel in the Private Security Industry (issued February 9, 2016)

**e. Lack of service assignment of a worker in a contracting or subcontracting arrangement**
   - For a continuous period of **three (3) months**
   - See Section 13 of Department Order No. 174, Series of 2017: Rules Implementing Articles 106 to 109 of the Labor Code, as Amended

> **Minimum Guarantee:** In no case will an employee get **less than one (1) month separation pay** if the separation is due to the above stated causes.

#### 2. One-Month Pay per Year of Service

An employee is entitled to separation pay equivalent to **his/her one-month pay for every year of service** (a fraction of at least six (6) months being considered as one whole year), if his/her separation from service is due to any of the following:

**a. Installation by employer of labor-saving devices**

**b. Redundancy**
   - When the position of the employee has been found to be **excessive or unnecessary** in the operation of the enterprise

**c. Impossible reinstatement**
   - Impossible reinstatement of the employee to his or her former position or to a substantially equivalent position
   - For reasons **not attributable to the fault of the employer**
   - Examples:
     - Reinstatement ordered by a competent authority cannot be implemented due to closure or cessation of operations of the establishment/employer
     - The position to which he or she is to be reinstated no longer exists
     - There is no substantially equivalent position in the establishment to which he or she can be assigned
   - See *Gaco vs. NLRC, et al.*, G.R. No. 104690, February 23, 1994

**d. Lack of service assignment of security guard by reason of age**
   - See DOLE Department Order No. 150, Series of 2016

### D. Computation

**Basis of Computation:**
The computation of separation pay of an employee shall be based on **his/her latest salary rate**.

**Integration of Allowances:**
It would be an error not to integrate the allowance with the basic salary. The **salary base properly used in computing the separation pay should include not just the basic salary but also the regular allowances** that an employee has been receiving.

> **Legal Precedent:** *Planters' Products, Inc. vs. NLRC*, G.R. No. 78524, January 20, 1989

**Formula:**

For **1/2 month pay per year**:
```
Latest Salary Rate × 0.5 × Number of Years of Service = Separation Pay
(where fraction of ≥6 months = 1 whole year)
```

For **1 month pay per year**:
```
Latest Salary Rate × 1 × Number of Years of Service = Separation Pay
(where fraction of ≥6 months = 1 whole year)
```

### E. Notice of Termination

The employer may terminate the employment of an employee due to the above-mentioned authorized causes by serving a **written notice** on:

1. **The employee**, AND
2. **The Department of Labor and Employment** through its regional office having jurisdiction over the place of business

**Timing:** At least **one (1) month before the intended date** thereof.

**How to Submit Notice:**

**Option 1: Manual Submission**
- Accomplish **RKS Form 5 of 2020**
- Submit the same to the **DOLE Provincial/Field Office** having jurisdiction over the workplace
- See DOLE Labor Advisory No. 17-A, Series of 2020: Establishment Report Form (issued June 11, 2020)

**Option 2: Online Submission**
- Accomplish and submit an online report at the **DOLE Establishment Report Systems**
- 🔗 **https://reports.dole.gov.ph/**

> **Reference:** DOLE Department Order No. 147, Series of 2015: Amending the Implementing Rules and Regulations of Book VI of the Labor Code of the Philippines, as amended (issued September 7, 2015)

### F. Coverage from Income Tax of Separation Pay

**Tax Exemption:**

Pursuant to **Section 32(B)(6)(b) of the Tax Code of 1997, as amended**, any amount received by an official or employee or by his heirs from the employer as a consequence of **separation** of such official or employee from the service of the employer due to:

- **Death**
- **Sickness**
- **Other physical disability**, OR
- **For any cause beyond the control of the said official or employee**

Shall:
- ✅ **Not be included in the gross income**, and
- ✅ Be **exempt from taxation** under Title II of the same Code

> **Additional Reference:** BIR Ruling No. 057-2014

## Summary of Authorized Causes and Separation Pay Amounts

| Authorized Cause | Separation Pay Amount |
|-----------------|----------------------|
| **1/2 Month Pay per Year:** | |
| • Retrenchment to prevent losses | 0.5 month × years of service |
| • Closure/cessation (not due to losses) | 0.5 month × years of service |
| • Employee disease (6+ months incurable) | 0.5 month × years of service |
| • Security guard: no assignment (6 months) | 0.5 month × years of service |
| • Subcontractor worker: no assignment (3 months) | 0.5 month × years of service |
| **1 Month Pay per Year:** | |
| • Installation of labor-saving devices | 1 month × years of service |
| • Redundancy | 1 month × years of service |
| • Impossible reinstatement (no fault of employer) | 1 month × years of service |
| • Security guard: age-related no assignment | 1 month × years of service |

> **Note:** Fraction of at least 6 months of service = 1 whole year
