# E. Computation of the Estimated Equivalent Monthly Rate (EEMR) of Monthly-Paid and Daily-Paid Employees

The following factors are based on the declared regular holidays and special non-working days in **Proclamation No. 42-22**, as amended by **Proclamation No. 90-22**.

## 1. For Monthly-Paid Employees

Factor **365 days in a year** is used in determining the equivalent annual and monthly salary of monthly-paid employees. To compute their Estimated Equivalent Monthly Rate (EEMR), the procedure is as follows:

### Formula:

```
EEMR = (Applicable Daily Rate (ADR) × 365) / 12 months
```

### Breakdown of 365 days/year:

| Component | Days |
|-----------|------|
| Ordinary working days | 293.0 |
| Rest days | 52.0 |
| Regular holidays | 12.0 |
| Special non-working days | 8.0 |
| **Total equivalent no. of days/year** | **365.0** |

---

## 2. For Daily-Paid Employees

The following factors and formula may be used in computing the EEMR of different groups of daily-paid employees for purposes of entitlement to minimum wages and allied benefits under existing laws:

### a. For those who are required to work everyday, including Sundays or rest days, special days and regular holidays

#### Formula:

```
EEMR = (Applicable Daily Rate (ADR) × 395.0) / 12 months
```

#### Breakdown of 394.4 days/year (rounded to 395.0):

| Component | Days |
|-----------|------|
| Ordinary working days | 293.0 |
| Rest days (52 × 1.3) | 67.6 |
| Regular holidays (12 × 2) | 24.0 |
| Special non-working days (8 × 1.3) | 10.4 |
| **Total equivalent no. of days/year** | **395.0** |

---

### b. For those who do not work and are not considered paid on Sundays or rest days

#### Formula:

```
EEMR = (Applicable Daily Rate (ADR) × 313) / 12 months
```

#### Breakdown of 313 days/year:

| Component | Days |
|-----------|------|
| Ordinary working days | 293.0 |
| Regular holidays | 12.0 |
| Special non-working days (10.4 if worked) | 8.0 |
| **Total equivalent no. of days/year** | **313ᵍ** |

**Note ᵍ:** Factor 305 may be used instead of 313 if the 8 special non-working days are not considered paid.

---

### c. For those who do not work and are not considered paid on Saturdays and Sundays or rest days

#### Formula:

```
EEMR = (Applicable Daily Rate (ADR) × 261) / 12 months
```

#### Breakdown of 261 days/year:

| Component | Days |
|-----------|------|
| Ordinary working days | 241 |
| Regular holidays | 12 |
| Special non-working days (10.4 if worked) | 8 |
| **Total equivalent no. of days/year** | **261ʰ** |

**Note ʰ:** Factor 253 may be used instead of 261 if the 8 special non-working days are not considered paid.

---

## Important Note

Without prejudice to existing company policies, practices and/or agreements, the above **formulas are merely suggestions** and may be used as guides in determining the equivalent monthly minimum wage rates.
